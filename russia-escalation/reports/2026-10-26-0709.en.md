## What changed

1. **Support for Ukraine: further confirmation of restoration, continuing air-defense shortage. Confirmed as a presidential statement:** on 25 October Zelenskyy described the intelligence-support and Starlink/Starshield problems as resolved, but Patriot missile deliveries as too slow. This continues the government reporting line on warnings from 20 October and connectivity from 24 October, not a second independently verified restoration. **Our inference:** counter-evidence to a wholesale withdrawal of support; durability, full coverage and the delivery balance remain unknown. [Presidential channel](https://t.me/V_Zelenskiy_official/21521).

2. **Maritime strikes: new claims, not a verified vessel count. Confirmed as Russian ministry statements; results unverified:** the ministry claimed a strike on a ship heading to Chornomorsk on the morning of 25 October, one heading to Odesa that afternoon, and another similar strike on the morning of 26 October. It did not name the ships. The evidence obtained cannot reliably establish their identities, whether they were distinct vessels, or their military cargoes. Reuters did not independently verify the first claim. All ministry reports constitute one belligerent source line; continued attacks on Ukrainian trade, or exaggerated reporting of results, are alternatives to a NATO test. [Ministry: morning of 25 October](https://t.me/mod_russia/68513), [afternoon](https://t.me/mod_russia/68530), [26 October](https://t.me/mod_russia/68548), [Reuters](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Russia%2Bsays%2Bit%2Bhit%2Ba%2Bcargo%2Bvessel%2Band%2BUkrainian%2Blogistic%2Bhubs%2Bovernight/27229261.html).

   **Khoryv is a separate, already recorded case. Confirmed:** Romanian warning ARN 155/2026, reporting an observation at **09:00 UTC on 25 October**, still describes a capsized floating hull and three missing people. This is not another attack, a completed sinking or a final rescue tally. [Romanian hydrographic authority](https://www.dhmfn.ro/ro/informatii-de-interes-public/avize-radio-pentru-navigatori/).

3. **Mobilization: one new local case in a still incomplete sample. Unverified witness claims:** Idite Lesom published two accounts of a check in the same St Petersburg shopping center on **23 October**.
