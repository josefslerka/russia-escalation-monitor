## What changed

1. **Contship Jet: sinking confirmed, attribution remains open. Confirmed:** [AP citing the maritime administration](https://apnews.com/article/2bfd978d36fbb958781cf2721cabcf5c) reports the sinking following the 28 October attack; all 18 crew members were rescued. [Bulgarian prosecutors](https://prb.bg/bg/news/aktualno/80101-voenno-okrazhna-prokuratura-sofiya-obrazuva-dosadebno-proizvodstvo-za-napadeniet) opened an investigation without naming a perpetrator. **Reported / expert inference:** according to [Reuters](https://gcaptain.com/greek-containership-sunk-by-naval-drones-off-bulgaria-as-black-sea-attacks-escalate/), Ambrey assesses the drone as probably a variant or derivative of the Russian Murena-300S design. Verified footage of an explosion does not identify who ordered it. **Our assessment:** this is a further stage of an incident already counted, not another attack. Design similarity increases suspicion but does not establish the operator or an intention to test NATO. An exclusive economic zone is not territorial waters; 40% is retained.

2. **An operational pause in Ukraine and messaging pressure on the Baltics. Reported, two distinct source lines:** [Reuters relays the WSJ](https://www.arabnews.pk/world/ukraine-pauses-refinery-strikes-after-trump-pressure-as-russian-attacks-kill-at-least-20-3005618) on a de facto pause in strikes on Russian refineries following US pressure; Reuters could not independently verify it. The WSJ cites at least two military sources and other unnamed people. Zelensky denies a formal ban. **Separate Reuters reporting**, based on [three European and one US official](https://uk.marketscreener.com/news/pentagon-tells-europeans-to-cool-their-rhetoric-on-russia-sources-say-ce785fdad081f627), describes US requests in preceding weeks for more restrained Baltic rhetoric. Officials have not publicly confirmed the account. **Our assessment:** the first claim concerns possible operational behavior, the second communication. Fuel prices, domestic politics and efforts to promote negotiations are alternatives to Russian-induced self-deterrence. These are neither two confirmations of the same claim nor evidence of an implemented troop withdrawal or reduction in Western deliveries.

3. **Oleshky: from preparation to a reported departure, final outcome unverified. Reported:** [Lubinets](https://t.me/dmytro_lubinetzs/12955) announced completed demining and a ready convoy on 29 October. [Reuters citing Izvestia](https://www.aol.com/articles/aid-convoy-sets-off-russian-043145000.html) reports an overnight departure on the morning of 30 October. This is an inherited source line, not independent verification of delivery. [A separate evacuation of five people](https://t.me/dmytro_lubinetzs/12954) is explicitly distinguished from Oleshky. **Our assessment:** a concrete local de-escalatory step that can coexist with warfare elsewhere. The number evacuated by this mission and completed aid distribution remained unverified at the cutoff.

4. **Energy: new damage alongside partial repairs. Confirmed as an operator report:** [Ukrenergo](https://t.me/Ukrenergo/5589) reported new outages in three regions on 29 October.
