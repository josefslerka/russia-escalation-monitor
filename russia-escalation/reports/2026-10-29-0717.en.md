## What changed

1. **Contship Jet: a new strike and crew rescue. Confirmed:** the [Bulgarian transport ministry](https://www.mtc.government.bg/bg/category/1/18-chlenniyat-ekipazh-na-konteynerovoza-contship-jet-e-spasen) announced the rescue of 18 people following a maritime-drone strike on 28 October. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/357e91838a3695acb39d3e361bc2fe72) reports the ministerial statement. The ship was sailing from Haifa to Constanța outside Bulgarian territorial waters. **Unknown:** perpetrator, any military cargo and the exact attack time; sources differ on timing. Today's [Maritime.bg](https://www.maritime.bg/157323/) reports a sinking from two anonymous sources, without official confirmation. **Our inference:** recurring maritime danger has increased, but a campaign against Ukrainian commercial shipping remains an alternative to a deliberate NATO test. The 40% estimate is retained.

2. **Russia's claim to have attacked shipping does not identify this vessel. Unverified claim:** the [Russian defence ministry](https://t.me/mod_russia/68618) reports this morning an overnight strike on an unnamed cargo ship heading to Odesa with military or dual-use cargo. The different date and destination do not permit identification as Contship Jet. A general claim is not an admission of this specific strike. **Khoryv is a different, older case:** the [report of a corvette remaining on station](https://www.maritime.bg/157268/) describes continued surveillance according to the navy commander, not another attack or a completed inspection.

One volunteer's allegation of Russian preparations for another attack remains unverified. **Our inference:** narrow counter-evidence to uninterrupted escalation everywhere, not a nationwide ceasefire or a completed mission.
