## Evidence against escalation

- Reported local ceasefire compliance and the completed diplomatic contact are concrete but narrowly bounded counterweights. A broader risk reduction requires verified implementation and follow-through.
- The UK and Poland agreed a defence plan on 27 October. November’s coalition exercise remains planned, not an initiated deployment in Ukraine. [UK Ministry of Defence](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-and-poland-strengthen-defences-on-europes-eastern-flank--2). This runs against the notion of a wholesale halt to support, but does not itself prove greater combat readiness today.
- Russia today released an announcement of a Bulava test under its training plan. **The ministry’s statement is confirmed, not independently verified execution.** [Original announcement](https://t.me/mod_russia/68595). Testing a delivery vehicle is not a nuclear explosion or, by itself, new crisis readiness. The post does not specify the exact launch date. Training is a credible alternative interpretation; independent convergence of unusual readiness and command changes was not obtained within incomplete coverage.
